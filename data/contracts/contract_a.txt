SUPPLIER SERVICE AGREEMENT

(SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - NOT A REAL CONTRACT.
Prepared for a reverse-engineering academic project. Party names are fictional.)

THIS AGREEMENT is made on 1 April 2026

BETWEEN

(1) ARANYA TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 12 Kasturi Road, Bengaluru 560001 (the "Customer"); and

(2) MERIDIAN FACILITY SERVICES LLP, a limited liability partnership registered under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, having its registered office at 4B Residency Road, Bengaluru 560025 (the "Supplier").

The Customer and the Supplier are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means the facility management, housekeeping and support services described in Schedule A.

1.2 "Charges" means the fees and other amounts payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the Services as set out in Schedule A.

1.3 "Invoice" means a valid invoice issued by the Supplier in accordance with Clause 7.1.

1.4 "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday on which commercial banks are open for general business in Bengaluru.

1.5 "Service Levels" means the service level commitments set out in Clause 9.1.

2. INTERPRETATION

2.1 Interpretation of Headings. Headings are inserted for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

2.2 Singular and Plural. Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa.

2.3 Reference to Clauses. In this Agreement, a reference to a Clause is to a clause of this Agreement.

2.4 "Payment Due Date" means, in respect of any Invoice, the date falling 30 (thirty) days after the date of that Invoice.

3. SCOPE OF SERVICES

3.1 Standard of Performance. The Supplier shall provide the Services with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with the Service Levels.

3.2 Service Levels Compliance. The Supplier shall perform the Services so as to achieve the Service Levels set out in Clause 9.1 and shall maintain the records necessary to demonstrate compliance.

3.3 Change Control. No change to the scope of the Services shall take effect unless agreed in writing by both Parties in accordance with Clause 17.1.

4. TERM

4.1 Initial Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 12 (twelve) months.

4.2 Renewal. This Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of 12 (twelve) months unless either Party gives notice in accordance with Clause 14.1.

5. CHARGES

5.1 Charges. The Customer shall pay the Charges set out in Schedule A.

5.2 Increase in Charges. The Supplier may increase the Charges once in any 12 (twelve) month period on 30 (thirty) days prior written notice in accordance with Clause 14.1, provided that no increase shall exceed 8% per annum.

6. TAXES

6.1 Taxes. All amounts payable under this Agreement are exclusive of applicable taxes, which shall be added to the relevant Invoice.

7. PAYMENT

7.1 Invoicing. The Supplier shall issue an Invoice monthly in arrears. Each Invoice shall state the amount due, the period covered and the tax amount.

7.2 Payment Period. The Customer shall pay the amount stated in each undisputed Invoice within 30 (thirty) days after the date of that Invoice (the "Payment Period"), and in any event not later than the Payment Due Date determined in accordance with Clause 2.4. Payment shall be made in Indian Rupees by electronic transfer to the account notified by the Supplier.

7.3 Disputed Invoices. The Customer may withhold payment of a genuinely disputed amount by giving written notice to the Supplier within 10 (ten) days of the Invoice, stating the grounds of dispute. The undisputed balance shall be paid in accordance with Clause 7.2.

8. LATE PAYMENT

8.1 Late Charges. If the Customer fails to pay any undisputed Invoice by the Payment Due Date determined in accordance with Clause 7.2, the Customer shall pay late charges at the rate of 1.5% per month on the overdue amount, calculated from the Payment Due Date until payment in full.

8.2 Interest. Without prejudice to the late charges payable under Clause 8.1, interest at the rate of 12% per annum shall accrue on any amount paid late, calculated from the Payment Due Date determined in accordance with Clause 7.2 until the date of payment.

8.3 Reasonableness. The Parties agree that the amounts payable under Clause 8.1 and Clause 8.2 are reasonable estimates of the cost of late payment and are not a penalty.

9. SERVICE LEVELS

9.1 Service Levels. The Supplier shall achieve a monthly service availability of not less than 99% for the Services, measured in accordance with Schedule B. Failure to achieve the Service Levels entitles the Customer to the service credits set out in Clause 9.2.

9.2 Service Credits. Where the Supplier fails to achieve the Service Levels, the Customer shall be entitled to service credits calculated in accordance with Schedule B, which shall be applied as a credit against the Charges payable under Clause 7.

10. DEFAULT AND CURE

10.1 Default. If the Customer fails to pay any undisputed Invoice and the late payment under Clause 8.1 continues after the expiry of the Cure Period specified in Clause 10.2, the Customer shall be in default.

10.2 Cure Period. If the Customer fails to pay any undisputed Invoice in accordance with Clause 7.2, the Customer shall have 15 (fifteen) days from receipt of written notice of the failure to cure that failure (the "Cure Period").

11. INDEMNITY

11.1 Indemnity. The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against all losses arising from the Supplier's wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality under Clause 17.2 or infringement of third party intellectual property rights.

12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 (thirty) days prior written notice in accordance with Clause 14.1.

12.2 Insolvency. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other Party commits an act of insolvency or is wound up.

12.3 Termination for Non-Payment. The Supplier may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the Customer remains in default under Clause 10.1 for a period exceeding 30 (thirty) days, provided that the Supplier has first served a notice on the Customer in accordance with Clause 14.1.

13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

13.1 Limitation of Liability. Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect or consequential loss, and the aggregate liability of each Party under this Agreement shall not exceed the Charges paid in the 12 (twelve) months preceding the claim.

14. NOTICES

14.1 Notices. Every notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand or by email to the address of the receiving Party set out in Schedule A, and shall be deemed served 7 (seven) days after despatch.

15. FORCE MAJEURE

15.1 Force Majeure. Neither Party shall be liable for any failure to perform caused by an event beyond its reasonable control, provided that it notifies the other Party promptly and uses reasonable endeavours to mitigate its effect.

16. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

16.1 Governing Law. This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it shall be governed by the laws of India.

16.2 Arbitration. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement that is not resolved by good faith negotiation within 30 (thirty) days shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in Bengaluru.

17. MISCELLANEOUS

17.1 Amendment. No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by an authorised representative of each Party. Agreement by email alone is not sufficient.

17.2 Confidentiality. Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other Party and shall not disclose it except as permitted by law.

17.3 Assignment. Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to a successor in connection with a merger or reorganisation.

17.4 Survival. Clauses which by their nature are intended to survive, including Clause 11, Clause 13, Clause 16 and this Clause 17.4, shall survive termination.

SCHEDULE A - SERVICES, CHARGES AND ADDRESSES
Services: facility management, housekeeping, front-desk and visitor management for the Customer's Bengaluru premises.
Charges: INR 4,50,000 per month, exclusive of taxes and reasonable expenses.
Addresses: as set out in the cover page of this Agreement.

SCHEDULE B - SERVICE LEVELS AND SERVICE CREDITS
Monthly availability: 99%. Service credits for availability below 99%: 5% of the monthly Charges for each full percentage point below 99%, capped at 25% of the monthly Charges. Availability measured as the ratio of time the Services are available to the total time in the calendar month.
