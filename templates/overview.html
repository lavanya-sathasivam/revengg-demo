What this workspace does

Contract Intelligence models a contract as linked obligations rather than a bag of text. Every finding points back to a clause, and the system says what it cannot determine instead of guessing.

Choose a workspace below. Conflict & Change Impact asks what breaks when a clause changes. Clause Explanation explains one stored clause and the authorities that may be relevant to it.
